Why I'm doing this in public

I've been off social media for almost ten years. Not because life was going well. Mostly because it wasn't, and I didn't want people to see.

Before that, I spent five years as a B2B sales trainer, winning top sales awards and coaching more than 100 people. I know how to help others grow. Now it's my turn to start again.

So instead of hiding, I'm sharing the whole climb: what I'm building, what I'm learning, and what it actually earns. No hype and no pretending. Just steady progress, shared honestly.

If you're quietly rebuilding too, you're welcome here.
