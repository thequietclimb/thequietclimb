FAQ常见问题

Questions, answered常见问题解答

Do you book hotels and tickets for me?你会帮我订酒店和门票吗？

No. You stay in control: your plan includes links, and you book and pay directly, so you always get the official price and confirmations in your own name. Where I know of promo codes or discounts, I'll include them.不会。由你自己掌控：行程里附有链接，你直接预订和付款，确保拿到官方价格，确认单也在你名下。如我知道有优惠码或折扣，会一并附上。

Is this a tour?这是旅行团吗？

No. It's free-and-easy: you explore on your own with a plan made by a local, plus an airport welcome and WhatsApp support on selected plans.不是。这是自由行：你带着本地人做的行程自己探索，指定方案还包含机场迎接和 WhatsApp 支援。

What's in my plan?行程里有什么？

A day-by-day itinerary with times, MRT and walking routes, map pins, food picks with what to order, booking links, rough costs, heat breaks and a rain plan for each day. English or 中文.每日行程，包括时间、地铁和步行路线、地图定位、美食推荐和点餐建议、预订链接、大概费用、避暑时段，以及每天的雨天方案。中英文都可以。

How long does it take to get my plan?多久能收到行程？

Usually 3 to 5 days after our planning call. Need it sooner? A 48-hour rush is +S$50. An Itinerary Check comes back within 24 hours.通常在规划通话后 3 至 5 天内。需要更快？48 小时加急 +S$50。行程检查 24 小时内回复。

How far ahead should I book?应该提前多久预约？

Two to four weeks before you fly is ideal, so there's time for the call, the plan and any changes. Flying sooner? Message me and I'll tell you honestly if I can fit it in.最好在出发前 2 至 4 周，这样有时间通话、规划和修改。时间更紧？联系我，我会如实告诉你能不能安排。

Is it good for kids, grandparents or anyone who can't walk far?适合小孩、长辈或不方便走远路的人吗？

Yes. Tell me in the form and I'll plan shorter walks, pram-friendly routes, lift access, rest stops and air-con breaks.适合。在表格里告诉我，我会安排较短的步行、适合推车的路线、有电梯的出入口、休息点和冷气休息时段。

How does the airport welcome work?机场迎接是怎样的？

On Plan + Welcome and above, I meet you at the Changi Airport arrival hall, hand you your printed trip folder and give you a 10-minute briefing on getting around. Send me your flight number and I'll track it."规划 + 机场迎接"及以上方案，我会在樟宜机场入境大厅接你，交给你纸本行程资料夹，并做 10 分钟出行简介。把航班号发给我，我会追踪航班。

What if it rains?下雨怎么办？

Rain conditions vary. Every day in your plan includes nearby indoor alternatives, and on the Support plan you can message me to rework the day.下雨情况不一定。行程每天都附有附近的室内备选；全程支援方案还可以随时联系我调整当天安排。

How do I pay?如何付款？

The Itinerary Check is paid in full by PayPal or card. For plans, a 50% deposit starts the planning and the rest is paid before the final plan is sent, by PayPal or international bank transfer.行程检查以 PayPal 或银行卡全额付款。行程方案先付 50% 订金开始规划，收到最终行程前付清余款，可用 PayPal 或国际银行转账。

What if I need to cancel?如果需要取消怎么办？

Full refund before planning starts. Once I've started planning, the deposit isn't refundable, but you'll still receive the plan. Full details on the cancellation and refund policy.开始规划前取消可全额退款。开始规划后订金不予退还，但你仍会收到行程。详情请看取消及退款政策。

Which languages do you speak?你说哪些语言？

English and Mandarin. Your plan, calls and WhatsApp support can be in either.英文和华语。行程、通话和 WhatsApp 支援都可以用任一语言。
