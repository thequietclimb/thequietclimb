Cancellation & refund policy取消及退款政策

Last updated: 27 November 2026最后更新：2026年11月27日

Plain and simple: if we haven't started, you get your money back.简单来说：我们还没开始，就全额退款。

Itinerary Check (S$29)行程检查（S$29）

  • Full refund if you cancel before we start checking your itinerary.在我们开始检查你的行程前取消，可全额退款。
  • Once we've started, it can't be refunded, but you'll still get your check and call.开始检查后不予退款，但你仍会收到检查结果和通话。
  • Upgrade later and your S$29 Itinerary Check fee is credited toward any of our three trip-planning packages (S$129, S$269 or S$399). It comes off your deposit.之后升级的话，这 S$29 行程检查费用可抵扣任何一个规划方案（S$129、S$269 或 S$399），直接从订金中扣除。

Plans (S$129, S$269, S$399)行程方案（S$129、S$269、S$399）

  • Full refund of your deposit if you cancel before planning starts (before our planning call).在开始规划前（规划通话前）取消，可全额退还订金。
  • Once planning has started, the deposit isn't refundable, but you'll still receive your plan.开始规划后，订金不予退还，但你仍会收到行程。
  • Once the final plan has been sent, the plan fee isn't refundable.最终行程发送后，方案费用不予退还。
  • If you cancel your trip after paying for a plan with an airport welcome or WhatsApp support, and you tell us at least 72 hours before you land, we refund the part of the price for the welcome and support you won't use. Message us and we'll confirm the amount.如果你已购买含机场迎接或 WhatsApp 支援的方案后取消旅行，并在抵达前至少 72 小时通知我们，我们会退还你不会使用的迎接及支援部分费用。联系我们，我们会确认金额。

Changing your dates更改日期

Trip moved? Tell us and we'll move your plan to the new dates for free if the days and places stay the same. Bigger changes count as a round of changes, or an extra day at S$40.旅行改期？告诉我们，如天数和地点不变，可免费调整到新日期。较大的改动算作一次修改，或按每天 S$40 加天收费。

If we can't deliver如果我们无法提供服务

If we can't deliver your plan or welcome for any reason on our side, you get a full refund for the part we couldn't deliver.如因我方原因无法提供行程或迎接服务，未能提供的部分将全额退款。

How refunds are paid退款方式

Refunds go back the way you paid (PayPal or bank transfer), usually within 7 working days. To cancel, message us on WhatsApp or by email.退款会按原付款方式（PayPal 或银行转账）退回，通常在 7 个工作日内完成。如需取消，请通过 WhatsApp 或电邮联系我们。
