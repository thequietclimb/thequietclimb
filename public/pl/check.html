What you get back: a sample你会收到什么：示例

Made-up example, not a real customer's plan.虚构示例，并非真实客户的行程。

The plan sent in (3 days):客人发来的行程（3 天）：

  • Day 1: Sentosa morning, Chinatown lunch, Sentosa afternoon第 1 天：上午圣淘沙，中午牛车水，下午又回圣淘沙
  • Day 2: Gardens by the Bay + paid light-show tickets第 2 天：滨海湾花园 + 付费灯光秀门票
  • Day 3: Orchard Road shopping, food court lunch S$15第 3 天：乌节路购物，美食广场午餐 S$15

The fixes (in writing, within 24 hours):修改建议（书面，24 小时内）：

  1. Stop zigzagging. Sentosa, then Chinatown, then back to Sentosa costs about 1.5 hours of travel. Keep Sentosa in one block and do Chinatown for dinner.别来回跑。圣淘沙、牛车水再回圣淘沙，路上大约多花 1.5 小时。把圣淘沙排在一起，牛车水改成晚餐。
  2. The light show is free. Garden Rhapsody at the Supertree Grove is free every night. The paid tickets are for other parts of the Gardens, not the show.灯光秀是免费的。擎天树的 Garden Rhapsody 每晚免费。付费门票是花园里其他景点的，不是灯光秀。
  3. Lunch should cost about S$5, not S$15. Eat at a hawker centre near Orchard Road: the same kind of food at a third of the price.午餐大约 S$5 就够，不用 S$15。去乌节路附近的小贩中心吃，同样的食物，三分之一的价钱。
  4. Shop after 3pm. Orchard Road is hottest and busiest at midday. Hawker lunch first, shops later when it's cooler.下午 3 点后再逛街。中午乌节路最热也最挤。先吃小贩中心，凉快些再逛。
  5. No beach at midday. The sun is strongest from 12 to 3pm. Do the beach before 11am or after 4pm, and an indoor spot like the Singapore Oceanarium in between.中午别去海滩。12 点到 3 点太阳最猛。海滩安排在 11 点前或 4 点后，中间去新加坡海洋馆（Singapore Oceanarium）等室内景点。
