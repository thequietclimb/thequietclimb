Privacy policy隐私政策

Last updated: 27 November 2026最后更新：2026年11月27日

Pocket Local Singapore (“Pocket Local”, “I”, “we”) plans trips in Singapore. This page explains what personal information we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. We follow Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).口袋本地通（Pocket Local Singapore，以下称“我们”）提供新加坡行程规划服务。本页说明我们收集哪些个人资料、用途，以及你的权利。我们遵守新加坡《2012年个人资料保护法》（PDPA）。

What we collect我们收集什么

  • What you tell us when you fill in the trip form or message us: your name, email, phone or WhatsApp number, where you're travelling from, travel dates, who is travelling (including children's ages), budget, interests, and notes such as diet or mobility needs.你填写行程表格或联系我们时提供的资料：姓名、电邮、电话或 WhatsApp 号码、出发地、旅行日期、同行人员（包括孩子年龄）、预算、兴趣，以及饮食或行动需求等备注。
  • Flight details if you choose an airport welcome.如选择机场迎接，你的航班资料。
  • Payment records (amount, date, payer name). Payments are processed by PayPal; we never see or store your full card details.付款记录（金额、日期、付款人姓名）。付款由 PayPal 处理，我们不会看到或储存你的完整银行卡资料。

How the trip form works行程表格如何运作

The form on our website doesn't store anything on our servers. When you press send, it opens WhatsApp on your device with your answers filled in, and nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to that chat.网站上的表格不会把资料存到我们的服务器。按下发送后，会在你的设备上打开 WhatsApp 并填好你的答案；在你于 WhatsApp 按发送之前，不会传送任何资料。该对话适用 WhatsApp 自身的隐私政策。

Why we use it使用目的

  • To plan your trip, talk to you about it and send you your plan.规划你的行程、与你沟通并发送行程。
  • To meet you at the airport, if your plan includes it.如方案包含机场迎接，用于接机。
  • To take payment and keep records we're required to keep.收款及保存法律要求的记录。
  • To send you travel tips or offers, only if you ask to receive them. You can stop at any time.仅在你要求时，发送旅行贴士或优惠。你可以随时取消。

Who we share it with与谁分享

We don't sell your personal information. We only share what's needed with the services we use to run Pocket Local: WhatsApp (messages), Google (email and documents for your plan) and PayPal (payments). We won't share your details with shops, hotels or attractions.我们不会出售你的个人资料。只会把必要的资料提供给我们运营所用的服务：WhatsApp（讯息）、Google（电邮及行程文件）和 PayPal（付款）。我们不会把你的资料分享给商家、酒店或景点。

Photos and reviews照片与评价

We will only post your photos, videos or review with your clear permission, and you can ask us to take them down at any time.只有在你明确同意后，我们才会发布你的照片、视频或评价，你也可以随时要求删除。

How long we keep it保存期限

We keep trip details for up to 12 months after your trip, in case you have questions or come back. Payment records are kept as long as the law requires. You can ask us to delete your details sooner.行程资料会在旅行结束后保存最多 12 个月，方便你日后查询或再次来访。付款记录按法律要求保存。你可以要求我们提早删除你的资料。

Your choices你的权利

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or withdraw your consent and have it deleted. Just message us and we'll reply within 30 days.你可以要求查看、更正我们持有的个人资料，或撤回同意并要求删除。联系我们即可，我们会在 30 天内回复。

Our website我们的网站

Our website saves your language choice (English or 中文) in your browser so the site remembers it. We also use the Meta Pixel, a small piece of code from Meta (Facebook and Instagram). It tells us when someone who saw our ads visits the site, taps to pay or sends a form, so we can measure and improve our ads. Meta may use cookies for this and processes the data under its own privacy policy. It doesn't give us your name or contact details. You can control ad tracking in your Facebook or Instagram ad settings and in your browser.网站会在你的浏览器中保存语言选择（English 或 中文），方便下次记住。我们也使用 Meta 像素（Meta Pixel），这是来自 Meta（Facebook 和 Instagram）的一小段代码，用来了解看过我们广告的人是否访问网站、点击付款或提交表格，以便衡量和改进广告效果。Meta 可能为此使用 Cookie，并按其隐私政策处理数据。它不会把你的姓名或联系方式提供给我们。你可以在 Facebook 或 Instagram 的广告设置以及浏览器中管理广告追踪。
