How we run Pocket Local, and how PH could too

A quick look at what we do in Singapore, so you can see how Pocket Local Philippines would work in Manila. Same idea, your city.

From Kev · pocketlocalsg.com

The idea

Pocket Local is a local friend for overseas families visiting our city. We plan their days around their kids, meet them at the airport, and stay on WhatsApp while they're here. They book their own hotel and tickets. We just make the days easy.

We don't call it a tour or a travel agency, because it isn't one. It's a local who knows where the clean toilets, cheap good food and shady spots are.

How a stranger becomes a client

  1. They see a useful postTips, photos and easy plans on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. Three a day on Facebook.
  2. They comment PLANFacebook sends them our free 1-day family plan by private message, automatically. Nothing is posted in public.
  3. They message usOnce they have the free plan, they start asking questions. That chat is where trust builds.
  4. We plan their tripEvery day planned around their dates, kids and pace, plus an airport welcome and WhatsApp support.
  5. They leave a reviewPhotos and a review from real families are what bring the next client.

Our house rules

Sound like a person

Short, casual, like a local typing on a phone. No sales talk.

No prices in Facebook posts

Talk about the day, not the money. Pricing happens in private chat.

Be useful first

Most posts are free tips. Only about one post a week asks for anything.

Real or credited photos

Our own photos, or free-licence ones with the photographer credited.

Keep home private

No home address, no family details beyond what we choose to share.

First family free

Our first family gets the full service free, in return for an honest review and photos we can use.
