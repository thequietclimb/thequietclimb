Singapore itinerary with kids: 4 easy days from a local dad

新加坡亲子游 · 4天行程 · By Kev, a Singaporean dad · 6 Nov 2026

A relaxed 4-day Singapore plan for families with young kids. One area per half day, indoor stops for the hot hours, and the free evening shows kids love.

Before you start: the one rule

Plan outdoor things before 11am or after 5pm. The middle of the day is hot, so that's when you go indoors: aquariums, museums, malls. Two areas a day is plenty with small kids.

Day 1 · Land, rest, then Gardens by the Bay

Arriving at Changi? Walk over to Jewel for the Rain Vortex, then get to your hotel and rest.

Around 6pm, head to Gardens by the Bay. Have dinner nearby, then catch the free Supertree light show at 7:45pm or 8:45pm. Lie on the ground and look up. It's the easiest first night there is.

Day 2 · Sentosa

Morning: S.E.A. Aquarium, indoors and cool.

Late afternoon: the beach. Palawan Beach is calm and good for little ones. Stay for sunset.

Day 3 · The zoo, early

Go to Singapore Zoo when it opens, while the animals are active and it's cooler. Pack swimwear: the zoo has a water play area that kids love.

Afternoon: back to the hotel pool. Tired kids, happy parents.

Day 4 · Katong and Marina Bay

Morning in Katong and Joo Chiat for the colourful shophouses and a bowl of Katong laksa.

Evening around Marina Bay: the Merlion, the waterfront, and a hawker dinner.

Getting around with kids

The MRT is clean, cold and easy with a stroller. You can tap in with a contactless Visa or Mastercard, no ticket needed. Grab (the ride-hailing app) is handy for short hops with tired kids.

Carry some cash: many hawker stalls don't take cards.

If it rains

Rain usually passes in 30 to 60 minutes. Duck into the nearest mall, most MRT stations connect straight into one, and carry on after.
