Half a day in Katong & Joo Chiat

加东与如切 · 半日游 · 1 Nov 2026

Colourful shophouses, Katong laksa and nyonya kueh. A side of Singapore most tourists miss.

Swipe for more →

Morning · The colourful houses

Peranakan shophouses along Koon Seng Road and Joo Chiat Road. Go in the morning when it's cooler, great for family photos.

Lunch · Katong laksa

Short rice noodles in spicy coconut broth, cut short so you eat it with just a spoon.

Snack · Nyonya kueh

Colourful Peranakan cakes. Get a mix to share, kids always go for the rainbow ones.

Getting there · Brown line, Marine Parade

You come out at Marine Parade Central, a real local neighbourhood with a hawker centre. Then cross the road into Katong. Too hot or raining? Parkway Parade mall is right there for air-con and shelter.
