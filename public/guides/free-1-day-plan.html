Free: 1 day in Singapore with kids

免费：带孩子玩新加坡一日行程 · 3 Nov 2026

Colourful Katong in the morning, a break from the heat, then satay and two free light shows at night.

Swipe for more →

Morning: Katong & Joo Chiat

Take the brown line to Marine Parade. See the colourful Peranakan shophouses, try nyonya kueh, then Katong laksa for lunch.

1:30pm: hide from the heat

Early afternoon is the hottest part of the day. Head to an aircon mall or back to the hotel for a nap or the pool.

Evening: satay & two free light shows

Dinner at Lau Pa Sat, blue line 1 stop to Bayfront, Spectra at Marina Bay Sands, then the Supertree light show at Gardens by the Bay.
