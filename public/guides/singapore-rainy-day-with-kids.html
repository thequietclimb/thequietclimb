Rainy day in Singapore with kids: what locals actually do

新加坡下雨天 · 带娃怎么玩 · By Kev, a Singaporean dad · 6 Nov 2026

Rain in Singapore usually comes and goes fast. Here's how locals handle it with kids, plus MRT stations that connect straight into a mall.

Don't cancel. Pause.

Most rain here is a heavy shower that passes in 30 to 60 minutes. Locals don't cancel the day. We pause somewhere dry, get a drink, and carry on.

MRT stations with a mall right there

These connect into a mall or are a short covered walk away, so you stay dry:

  • City & Orchard: Orchard (ION Orchard), Somerset (313@Somerset), Dhoby Ghaut (Plaza Singapura), City Hall (Raffles City), Bugis (Bugis Junction), Esplanade and Promenade (Suntec City, Marina Square), HarbourFront (VivoCity)
  • East: Paya Lebar (Paya Lebar Quarter), Bedok (Bedok Mall), Tampines (Tampines Mall)
  • North & West: Bishan (Junction 8), Ang Mo Kio (AMK Hub), Serangoon (NEX), Jurong East (JEM)

Indoor places kids love

  • S.E.A. Aquarium on Sentosa
  • The Flower Dome and Cloud Forest at Gardens by the Bay
  • ArtScience Museum at Marina Bay
  • Jewel at Changi Airport, with the Rain Vortex
  • National Museum of Singapore

Small things that help

An umbrella does double duty here: rain and sun. Pack a light change of clothes for the kids. Malls are cold inside, so bring a thin jacket.
