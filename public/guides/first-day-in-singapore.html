Your first day in Singapore after landing, with kids

落地新加坡第一天 · 亲子攻略 · By Kev, a Singaporean dad · 6 Nov 2026

A simple checklist for families arriving in Singapore: what to do before you fly, at Changi, and on your first evening.

Before you fly: the SG Arrival Card

Everyone entering Singapore, kids included, submits an SG Arrival Card. It's free on the official ICA website or the MyICA app, and you can do it up to 3 days before you arrive.

Watch out for look-alike sites that charge a fee. The official one is free. Rules can change, so check the ICA website before you travel.

At Changi

Changi is easy with kids. If you have time and energy, walk over to Jewel to see the Rain Vortex.

Getting to the city: taxi or Grab is simplest with luggage and tired kids. The MRT also runs from the airport if you're travelling light.

Money and phones

Contactless Visa or Mastercard works for the MRT and most shops. Carry some cash for hawker stalls. Sort out an eSIM or local SIM so you can use maps and Grab from the start.

Your first evening: keep it easy

Don't plan a big first day. Rest, have an early dinner at a hawker centre, and if the kids still have energy, the free Supertree light show at Gardens by the Bay runs at 7:45pm and 8:45pm.
