How to order prawn noodle like a Singaporean

新加坡虾面怎么点才地道 · 9 Nov 2026

Soup or dry, pick your noodle, and how a local orders it. Plus the free soup top-up most stalls give.

Swipe for more →

First · Soup or dry?

Dry comes tossed in chilli sauce or ketchup, with the soup on the side. Either way, the rich prawn broth is the whole point.

Then · Pick your noodle

Thick yellow noodle, bee hoon, or kuey tiao (the flat white noodle). You can mix, e.g. bee hoon + noodle.

My order

Dry, yellow noodle / bee hoon, no chilli, no bean sprouts. That's my taste. Most Singaporeans like chilli.

Tip · Soup top-up

Most stalls will top up your soup once. Ask more than once and you might get a stare.

Where to go

You'll find a good one at most hawker centres.
